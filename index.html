1. Introduction

Welcome to InstaSEO AI ("we," "our," or "the App"), developed and owned by Imperial Tech O.P.C.. We are dedicated to maintaining the trust and privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application (Package Name: com.aistudio.instaseo.rxqz).

By downloading, installing, or accessing InstaSEO AI, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Information Collection & Processing

InstaSEO AI operates with a privacy-by-design framework. We do not require users to create an account, log in with social credentials, or submit personal identification documents.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Instagram URLs and Usernames: Entered by you to perform public SEO audits, index checks, and retention signal evaluations.
  • Content Drafts and Keywords: Topic titles, captions, and draft text submitted to our generation tools (Caption Engine, Hashtags, Viral Hooks, Keyword Explorer).
  • Selected Media Files (Images and Videos): Visual media chosen via the standard Android Photo Picker for multimodal content analysis. These media files are processed transiently solely to compute algorithmic recommendations.

B. Automatically Stored Local Data

  • Local Database (Room / SQLite): Bookmarked audits, saved hashtags, and generated captions are stored directly on your physical device. Imperial Tech O.P.C. does not host or retain private databases of your personal drafts.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob

Advertisements in the App: InstaSEO AI displays Banner Ads and Interstitial Ads served via Google AdMob to support free access to our algorithmic tools.

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) may collect and process device-specific and diagnostic data to serve ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): A unique, user-resettable identifier for ad attribution and frequency capping.
  • Device Information: Device brand, model, operating system version, screen resolution, and carrier.
  • Ad Performance & Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, format performance, and error logs.
  • General Location Data: Non-precise, coarse location derived from IP address for regional ad delivery.

For more details on how Google handles advertisement data, please refer to:

  • Google Advertising & Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.

4. AI & Cloud Diagnostics (Google Gemini)

AI Processing Notice: Features like Content Diagnostics, Caption Generation, and Algorithmic Audits utilize Google Cloud and Gemini AI APIs.
  • Content entered for generation is transmitted via secure, encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels to computing endpoints solely to generate the requested output.
  • User inputs are not used to train public machine learning models.
  • No facial recognition, biometric scans, or audio surveillance are performed.

5. Device Permissions

InstaSEO AI requests only the essential system permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Needed to connect to AI generation endpoints and load AdMob advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Needed to check if the device is connected to the internet before making network calls.
  • Photo & Video Access: Utilizes the official Android Photo Picker (zero broad storage permissions required). The App cannot access other files on your device without explicit selection.

6. Data Control and Deletion

You have full autonomy over your saved records:

  • Delete individual saved items inside the "Saved Library" tab.
  • Wipe all app data anytime via: Settings > Apps > InstaSEO AI > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately purges all locally stored data.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted towards children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has provided information through the App, please reach out to us for immediate remediation.

8. Data Security

Imperial Tech O.P.C. employs industry-standard measures to secure your usage:

  • End-to-end TLS encryption for all network transmissions.
  • No user accounts, banking credentials, or personal passwords are collected.
